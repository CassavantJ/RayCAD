NIST test files (sources nist-pmi, nist-d2mi and nist-mtc in manifest.json)

These STEP files were made by employees of the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), an agency
of the US Government, in the course of their official duties. Under 17 U.S.C. 105 they are not subject to
copyright protection in the United States and are in the public domain there.

- PMI test cases (CTC, FTC, STC): the NIST MBE PMI Validation and Conformance Testing project page,
  https://www.nist.gov/ctl/smart-connected-systems-division/smart-connected-manufacturing-systems-group/mbe-pmi-0,
  states that the test cases, CAD models and STEP files can be used without any restrictions. NIST asks for
  acknowledgement when they are used, and its logo may not be used in promotional material. The same statement covers
  the copy of the STEP-file zip in NIST's own STEP File Analyzer repository, https://github.com/usnistgov/SFA.
- NIST/MTC box assembly (box, cover, plate; fetch-only, not in this repository): modelled at NIST and listed among the
  CAD models on the PMI project page above; the STEP files come from NIST's repository
  https://github.com/usnistgov/AR-PMI, and the box assembly dataset (DOI 10.18434/T4/1503154) is published under
  https://www.nist.gov/open/license.
- D2MI models: the README in NIST-D2MI-Models.zip states the public-domain status above and that NIST makes no
  guarantees about the files.
